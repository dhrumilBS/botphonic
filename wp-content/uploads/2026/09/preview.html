Capabilities

Everything the AI voice agent does before, during and after a call

Not a call menu with a nicer voice. Each capability below is live in the platform today and controlled from one dashboard.

AI call management

The agent handles the whole conversation on inbound and outbound calls, and knows when a human should take over.

  • Spam and robocall screening
  • Human-like conversation, under 300 ms
  • Inbound and outbound on one agent

Scheduling and data sync

Checks live availability, books and confirms the slot, then writes the appointment and contact straight back to your systems.

  • Google Calendar, Outlook, Calendly
  • Contact updated in your CRM
  • Reminders that cut no-shows

AI sales automation

Qualifies prospects on the call, scores the lead in real time and pushes it into your pipeline before a rep picks up.

  • Budget, timeline and intent captured
  • Real-time lead scoring
  • Scored lead pushed to your CRM

Outbound batch calling

Runs reminder, renewal and follow-up campaigns at any volume, with live pickup detection and per-call outcome tracking.

  • Thousands of calls in parallel
  • Branded caller ID, TCPA controls
  • Outcomes logged to your CRM

Post-call actions

Turns the finished call into a summary and transcript, then updates your CRM and notifies your team automatically.

  • Recording, transcript and summary
  • CRM record updated instantly
  • Sentiment and QA scoring

Email and SMS follow-up

One agent, one thread. It follows up after the call across phone, WhatsApp, SMS and email so nothing slips.

  • Automated SMS and email follow-up
  • WhatsApp and voice in one thread
  • Scheduled re-engagement
